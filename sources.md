### 3. US, Mexico and Bolivia lose measles-elimination status (Americas, health)
- Fact: PAHO said endemic measles transmission has been re-established in Bolivia, Mexico and the United
  States, which therefore lose elimination status (commission met 10-13 Nov; Director Jarbas Barbosa accepted).
  Americas: 58,213 confirmed cases and 82 deaths in 2026 as of 13 Nov. US had held the status since 2000.
- Date evidence: PAHO press release dated 17-11-2026; NBC News, The Hill, Health Policy Watch, AFP via France 24.
- Links: https://www.paho.org/en/news/17-11-2026-paho-urges-countries-step-efforts-stop-measles-outbreaks-and-move-towards ;
